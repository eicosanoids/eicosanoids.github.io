Cytosolic phospholipase A2 cuts arachidonic acid from the sn-2 position of membrane phosphatidylcholine, the first step of eicosanoid production in most cells. Two forms are drawn, cPLA2α (PLA2G4A), the main one, and cPLA2γ (PLA2G4C). How cPLA2α is activated, by Ca2+ and by phosphorylation, is drawn in the MRegAtlas map [Eicosanoids (generic)](https://metabolismregulation.github.io/atlas/#eicosanoids).
