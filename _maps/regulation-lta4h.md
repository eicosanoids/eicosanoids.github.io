Leukotriene A4 hydrolase makes leukotriene B4, and it carries a second, unrelated activity as an aminopeptidase. Two things hold the first activity down. A phosphorylation on serine 415 switches off the epoxide hydrolase and leaves the aminopeptidase working, which is why the endothelium holds a silent form of the enzyme, and protein phosphatase 1 switches it back on (Rybina 1997, Mueller 1999). The kinase was never identified, so the map draws that step without one.

The second brake is the substrate itself. During turnover the epoxide of leukotriene A4 binds covalently to tyrosine 378, one molecule per enzyme, and the enzyme is then dead for both of its activities. Replacing that tyrosine gives an enzyme that resists the suicide reaction and works two and a half times faster (Mueller 1995, Mueller 1996). The lower part of the map draws that reaction, with the enzyme and its substrate repeated.

The serine 415 work comes from one laboratory and has not been repeated elsewhere.
