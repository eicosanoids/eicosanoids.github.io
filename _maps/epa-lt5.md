5-lipoxygenase with FLAP turns EPA into 5(S)-HpEPE, which is reduced to 5(S)-HEPE or made into LTA5. LTA4 hydrolase makes LTB5 and LTC4 synthase makes LTC5, both shown in human neutrophils (Lee 1984). LTB5 is a weak, partial agonist compared with LTB4. The 12- and 15-lipoxygenase products of EPA have their own map.

Predicted steps, drawn with dashed arrows: LTD5 and LTE5 from LTC5, 5-oxo-EPE and 5,20-diHEPE. Each runs on an enzyme shown for the arachidonic acid product, with EPA as substrate measured in a database entry or not at all.
