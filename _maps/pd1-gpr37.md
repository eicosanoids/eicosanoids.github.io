Protectin D1, also called neuroprotectin D1, binds GPR37 on macrophages and raises calcium in cells that carry the receptor (Bang 2018). Through it the mediator drives the phagocytosis that clears zymosan and neutrophils from an inflamed paw, and mice without the receptor resolve inflammatory pain late. The receptor is not on microglia, so the effect belongs to the macrophages. Which G protein carries the calcium signal is not established, so the map holds the binding step alone.
