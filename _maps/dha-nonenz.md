Free radicals oxidise DHA without enzymes into the F4-neuroprostanes, abundant in the brain where DHA is enriched (Roberts 1998). Peroxidation of DHA also gives the aldehyde 4-hydroxy-2-hexenal (4-HHE), the n-3 counterpart of 4-HNE (Guichardant 2004).
