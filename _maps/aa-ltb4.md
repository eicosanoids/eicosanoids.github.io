5-lipoxygenase (ALOX5) with its activating protein FLAP (ALOX5AP) oxygenates arachidonic acid to 5(S)-HpETE and turns it into LTA4. LTA4 hydrolase (LTA4H) makes LTB4. Neutrophils inactivate LTB4 by ω-oxidation: CYP4F3 and related P450s hydroxylate it to 20-hydroxy-LTB4, which is oxidised further to 20-oxo- and 20-carboxy-LTB4, the last step by CYP4F3 or ALDH3A2. The prostaglandin reductase PTGR1, also known as LTB4 12-hydroxydehydrogenase, makes 12-oxo-LTB4.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. A common haplotype of the FLAP gene roughly doubles the risk of myocardial infarction and stroke and goes with higher LTB4 production by neutrophils, which ties this branch to inflammation in the arterial wall (Helgadottir 2004). In antibody-driven arthritis the receptor BLT1 on neutrophils is needed to start and to keep up their entry into the joint, and blocking it reverses established disease (Kim 2006).

The same signal is also needed to end the response: without BLT1 neutrophils do not die and get cleared, and Lyme arthritis does not resolve (Hilliard 2020). So this is one mediator that both starts inflammation and shuts it down, and which of the two is seen depends on the stage.
