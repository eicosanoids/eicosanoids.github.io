Leukotriene C4 synthase joins glutathione to leukotriene A4, the step that opens the cysteinyl leukotriene branch. A phosphorylation on serine 36 shuts it down. The kinase is p70S6 kinase, which works downstream of protein kinase C and of PGE2 acting on its own receptors, and the enzyme carrying the mark keeps about a fifth of its activity, because the first luminal loop is pulled onto the neighbouring subunit and the glutathione thiolate is no longer held in place (Ahmad 2016). In monocytes and macrophages meeting a microbial stimulus this is how the cell damps its own cysteinyl leukotriene output, and blocking p70S6 kinase with rapamycin doubles the activity (Ahmad 2011).

The map draws no phosphatase, because none is known for this site.
