n-3 docosapentaenoic acid (DPA) is turned by the same lipoxygenases as DHA into its own resolvins, protectins and maresins: 15-lipoxygenase makes 17-HpDPA, the start of resolvin D5 n-3 DPA and protectin D1 n-3 DPA, and 12-lipoxygenase makes 14-HpDPA, the start of maresin 1 n-3 DPA (Dalli 2013). Resolvins D1 and D2 of the n-3 DPA series come from the same 17-hydroperoxide, and P450 enzymes make its epoxides and their diols. A further series, the 13-series resolvins RvT1 to RvT4, is made in two cells: COX-2 in endothelial cells turns DPA into 13R-HDPA, and neutrophil 5-lipoxygenase completes it (Dalli 2015).
