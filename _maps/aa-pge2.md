Cyclooxygenases (PTGS1 and PTGS2) turn arachidonic acid into PGG2 and then PGH2, the shared precursor of the prostanoids, with 11(R)-HETE as a side product. Prostaglandin E synthases, microsomal PTGES and PTGES2 and cytosolic PTGES3, isomerise PGH2 to PGE2. 15-hydroxyprostaglandin dehydrogenase (HPGD) inactivates PGE2 to 15-keto-PGE2, which the prostaglandin reductases PTGR1 and PTGR2 reduce further. Tetranor-PGEM is its main urinary metabolite. Without enzymes PGE2 dehydrates to PGA2, which isomerises through PGC2 to PGB2. CYP4F8 hydroxylates PGH2 at carbon 19, and PTGES acting on PGG2 gives 15-hydroperoxy-PGE2.
