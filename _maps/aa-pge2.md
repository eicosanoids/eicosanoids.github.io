Cytosolic phospholipase A2, cPLA2α (PLA2G4A) and cPLA2γ (PLA2G4C), cuts arachidonic acid from membrane phosphatidylcholine. How cPLA2α is activated, by Ca2+ and by phosphorylation, is drawn in the MRegAtlas map [Eicosanoids (generic)](https://mreg-atlas.github.io/atlas/#eicosanoids). Cyclooxygenases (PTGS1 and PTGS2) turn arachidonic acid into PGG2 and then PGH2, the shared precursor of the prostanoids, with 11(R)-HETE as a side product. Prostaglandin E synthases, microsomal PTGES and PTGES2 and cytosolic PTGES3, isomerise PGH2 to PGE2. 15-hydroxyprostaglandin dehydrogenase (HPGD) inactivates PGE2 to 15-keto-PGE2, which the prostaglandin reductases PTGR1 and PTGR2 reduce further. Tetranor-PGEM is its main urinary metabolite. Without enzymes PGE2 dehydrates to PGA2, which isomerises through PGC2 to PGB2. CYP4F8 hydroxylates PGH2 at carbon 19, and microsomal PTGES turns 19-hydroxy-PGH2 into 19-hydroxy-PGE2, the main prostaglandin of human seminal fluid, made in the epithelium of the seminal vesicles (Stark 2005), and PTGES acting on PGG2 gives 15-hydroperoxy-PGE2.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. The clearest human evidence is on the clearance side: people with loss-of-function mutations in the dehydrogenase that oxidises PGE2 have chronically raised PGE2 and develop primary hypertrophic osteoarthropathy, so too little breakdown is itself a disease (Uppal 2008). A Crohn disease locus works by changing how much of the EP4 receptor is made, which places the same mediator in intestinal inflammation and in keeping the barrier intact (Libioulle 2007).

The direction is not fixed. Pooled trial data show that daily aspirin lowers long-term death from colorectal and other cancers, which is the firmest human evidence that this axis promotes tumours (Rothwell 2011). Yet blocking the same dehydrogenase pharmacologically raises PGE2 in tissue and speeds the regeneration of bone marrow, colon and liver (Zhang 2015). So the axis is harmful in cancer and in that bone disease and useful for repair, and any reading of the map has to say which tissue and which moment is meant.
