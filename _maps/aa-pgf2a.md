PGF2α comes from PGH2 by reduction, through the aldo-keto reductases AKR1A1 and AKR1C3 and the PGF synthase PRXL2B, and from PGE2 through carbonyl reductase CBR1. HPGD oxidises PGF2α to 15-keto-PGF2α, which CBR1 can reduce back, and 15-keto-PGE2 gives 15-keto-PGF2α through CBR1 as well. The prostaglandin reductases PTGR1 and PTGR2 reduce 15-keto-PGF2α to its 13,14-dihydro form, and CBR1 reduces that to 13,14-dihydro-PGF2α.
