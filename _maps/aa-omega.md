Cytochrome P450 ω-hydroxylases of the CYP4A and CYP4F families (CYP4A11, CYP4F2, CYP4F3) make 20-HETE, a vasoconstrictor in the kidney and the vessel wall. Other P450s hydroxylate arachidonic acid one to four carbons from its end, giving 19-HETE (CYP2E1, CYP2J2) and 16-, 17- and 18-HETE.
