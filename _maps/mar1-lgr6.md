Maresin 1, the macrophage mediator made from docosahexaenoic acid, activates LGR6 in a screen of more than two hundred G protein-coupled receptors, and the activation is specific for the natural stereoisomer (Chiang 2019). The receptor sits on phagocytes, and maresin 1 acting on it raises phagocytosis and the clearance of apoptotic cells and phosphorylates ERK and the cyclic AMP response element-binding protein. Overexpressing the receptor amplifies these actions and silencing it lowers them. The G protein is not established, so the map holds the binding step alone.
