The two essential fatty acids are elongated and desaturated by the same enzymes. The Δ6 desaturase FADS2 acts first, on linoleic acid and on α-linolenic acid (Cho 1999, Δ6), ELOVL5 elongates the products (Moon 2008), and the Δ5 desaturase FADS1 makes arachidonic acid and eicosapentaenoic acid (Cho 1999, Δ5). ELOVL2 elongates EPA to n-3 docosapentaenoic acid (Leonard 2002). Docosahexaenoic acid is not made by a Δ4 desaturase. The chain is elongated to 24 carbons, desaturated and then shortened again by peroxisomal β-oxidation, which needs acyl-CoA oxidase and D-bifunctional protein (Su 2001). Mice without ELOVL2 accumulate n-3 docosapentaenoic acid (Pauter 2014).
