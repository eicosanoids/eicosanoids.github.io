Thromboxane A2 binds the TP receptor (TBXA2R), cloned from human platelets and placenta, and opens a calcium-activated current through Gq and phospholipase C (Hirata 1991). The two isoforms bind the ligand alike and act on adenylyl cyclase in opposite directions, TPα raising cyclic AMP and TPβ lowering it (Hirata 1996). Thromboxane A3 from eicosapentaenoic acid acts on this receptor about as strongly as thromboxane A2 (Woodward 2011).
