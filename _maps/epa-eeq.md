Cytochrome P450 epoxygenases CYP2C8, CYP2C9 and CYP2J2 epoxidise EPA, mostly at its last double bond, giving 17,18-EEQ, with about the efficiency they show for arachidonic acid (Barbosa-Sicard 2005). 14,15-, 11,12- and 8,9-EEQ form too. Soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) turns the epoxides into diols (Fleming 2011).

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. These epoxides cut both ways, and the tissue decides. 17(R),18(S)-EEQ protects heart muscle cells against calcium overload at one to two nanomolar (Falck 2011), while the n-3 epoxides made by CYP2C8 promote pathological new vessel growth in the retina (Shao 2014). The hydrolysis step drawn here is what shortens their action.
