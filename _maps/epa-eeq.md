Cytochrome P450 epoxygenases CYP2C8, CYP2C9 and CYP2J2 epoxidise EPA, mostly at its last double bond, giving 17,18-EEQ, with about the efficiency they show for arachidonic acid (Barbosa-Sicard 2005). 14,15-, 11,12- and 8,9-EEQ form too. Soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) turns the epoxides into diols (Fleming 2011).
