Free radicals oxidise arachidonic acid without enzymes, mostly while it is still esterified in phospholipids, and give the F2-isoprostanes, among them 8-iso-PGF2α and 8,12-iso-iPF2α-VI. β-oxidation shortens 8-iso-PGF2α to 2,3-dinor-8-iso-PGF1α, which is measured in urine as a marker of oxidative stress.
