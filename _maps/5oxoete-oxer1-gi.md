The 5-lipoxygenase product 5-oxo-ETE acts on its own receptor, OXER1, cloned as the orphan receptor TG1019 (Hosoi 2002). The receptor couples to Gi and Go, so 5-oxo-ETE lowers cyclic AMP, and the fall is abolished by pertussis toxin. 5-oxo-ETE is the most potent agonist of the receptor, while docosahexaenoic acid and eicosapentaenoic acid act on it as antagonists, which is one way the omega-3 fatty acids damp a branch of the 5-lipoxygenase pathway (Hosoi 2002). The receptor draws and activates eosinophils and neutrophils (Bäck 2014).
