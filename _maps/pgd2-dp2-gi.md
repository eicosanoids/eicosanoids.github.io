PGD2 acts on a second receptor besides DP1, called DP2 or CRTH2 (PTGDR2), which draws T helper 2 cells, eosinophils and basophils and raises calcium through Gi (Hirai 2001). Its signalling is therefore opposite in sign to DP1, which works through Gs (Woodward 2011).
