Resolvin D1, made from docosahexaenoic acid, binds human phagocytes at specific sites, and a screen of receptors found two that respond to it, the lipoxin A4 receptor ALX (FPR2) and the orphan receptor GPR32 (Krishnamoorthy 2010). Overexpressing either raises the macrophage phagocytosis that resolvin D1 stimulates, and knocking either down lowers it. The actions of resolvin D1 on neutrophils are sensitive to pertussis toxin, but which G protein GPR32 itself uses is not established, so the map holds the binding step alone.
