Cytochrome P450 epoxygenases of the CYP2C and CYP2J families epoxidise DHA, mostly at its last double bond, giving 19,20-EDP and the other epoxydocosapentaenoic acids, and soluble epoxide hydrolase turns them into diols (Arnold 2010). CYP4F enzymes hydroxylate DHA at its end to 22-HDHA, and 21-HDHA forms as well. The EDPs inhibited angiogenesis and tumour growth in one study (Zhang 2013) and increased pathological neovascularisation in the retina in another (Shao 2014).
