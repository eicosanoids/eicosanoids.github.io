## Description

The cysteinyl leukotriene receptor CysLT2 is expressed by TRPV1 and P2X3 positive sensory neurons. LTC4 alone causes no pain, but it strengthens the pain evoked through the ATP-gated channel P2X3 (Okubo 2010).

## Colour

Red raises pain, blue relieves it, and olive acts both ways. Each molecule is coloured by its role in this block.

LTC4, CysLT2 and P2X3 are red.
