BLT2 (LTB4R2) is the second, low-affinity receptor for LTB4 (Yokomizo 2000). Its preferred natural ligand is 12-HHT, which binds it better than LTB4 does and draws mast cells (Okuno 2008). 12-HHT comes from the prostanoid branch, where thromboxane synthase makes it beside thromboxane, and most of it is made without that enzyme (Matsunobu 2013).

12-HHT joins the prostanoid and leukotriene branches of the atlas: it is drawn as a product in the PGI2, TXA2 and 12-HHT map and as a ligand here.
