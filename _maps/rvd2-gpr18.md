Resolvin D2 binds GPR18 on human neutrophils, monocytes and macrophages, with a dissociation constant near 10 nM, the range in which the mediator works (Chiang 2015). In human macrophages the pairing raises cyclic AMP and increases the clearance of bacteria and of apoptotic neutrophils, and mice without the receptor lose the effects of resolvin D2 in infection and in organ protection. The rise in cyclic AMP points to a stimulatory G protein, and because the coupling is not established the map holds the binding step alone.
