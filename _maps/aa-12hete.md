Platelet 12-lipoxygenase (ALOX12) and 15-lipoxygenase (ALOX15) make 12(S)-HpETE, which glutathione peroxidases GPX1 and GPX4 reduce to 12(S)-HETE. 12(S)-HpETE also rearranges to the hepoxilins A3 and B3, which soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) opens to the trioxilins, and 5-lipoxygenase and 12-lipoxygenase together make 5(S),12(S)-diHpETE. In the skin, 12R-lipoxygenase (ALOX12B) makes 12(R)-HpETE and 12(R)-HETE.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. The skin branch of this map is a genetic disease: mutations in both copies of ALOX12B or ALOXE3 are the second most common cause of autosomal recessive congenital ichthyosis (Hotz 2021). Mice without eLOX3 die soon after birth with a broken barrier and almost no hepoxilins, which places the defect at the step that makes them (Krieg 2013).

Which enzyme makes 12-HETE decides the direction. In pancreatic islets, deleting platelet-type 12-lipoxygenase makes oxidative stress in the beta cell worse, because 12/15-lipoxygenase rises instead, while deleting that second enzyme protects (Conteh 2019). A reader of this map should therefore keep the two enzymes apart and not treat 12-HETE as one signal.
