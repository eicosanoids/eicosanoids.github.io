Three proteins that move eicosanoids or take them apart, and the marks that decide what they can do.

The prostaglandin transporter SLCO2A1 brings PGE2 into the cell. Taking away its three N-glycans does not change how tightly it binds PGE2, but only a quarter of it reaches the cell surface, so the cell takes up much less. The sugar is not decoration here, it is what gets the carrier to the membrane (Nakamura 2023).

Membrane dipeptidase, which turns leukotriene D4 into leukotriene E4, is held on the outside of the cell by a lipid anchor. A phospholipase C cuts that anchor and the enzyme leaves the surface as a soluble protein, which happens by itself in the kidney and on command after insulin in fat cells (Park 2001, Movahedi 1997). The map draws no enzyme on that step, because the phospholipase was never identified.

Glutathione peroxidase 1 reduces the hydroperoxides the lipoxygenases make. The kinases c-Abl and Arg bind a proline-rich stretch of it and phosphorylate tyrosine 96, which raises its activity (Cao 2003). An O-linked sugar on its other end is needed for that binding and for the activation in high glucose, which is not drawn here (Yang 2009).
