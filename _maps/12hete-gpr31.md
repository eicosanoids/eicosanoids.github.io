The 12-lipoxygenase product 12(S)-HETE binds the orphan receptor GPR31 with high affinity, 4.8 nM, and the binding is specific for the 12(S) form (Guo 2011). Through this receptor 12(S)-HETE activates ERK1 and ERK2, MEK and NF-kappaB, and knocking the receptor down blocks the cell invasion that 12(S)-HETE stimulates. Which G protein carries the signal is not established, so the map holds the binding step alone.
