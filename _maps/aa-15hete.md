15-lipoxygenase (ALOX15) makes 15(S)-HpETE, which GPX1 reduces to 15(S)-HETE. ALOX15 also turns 15(S)-HpETE into eoxin A4 (14,15-LTA4), which LTC4 synthase conjugates with glutathione to eoxin C4. Exported, it is trimmed to eoxins D4 and E4 by the same enzymes as the cysteinyl leukotrienes. Soluble epoxide hydrolase (EPHX2) hydrolyses eoxin A4 to 14(R),15(S)-diHETE, shown with the purified mouse enzyme. Further products of 15(S)-HpETE are 15-oxo-ETE and the double oxygenation products, among them 5(S),15(S)-diHETE made with 5-lipoxygenase.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. The eoxins drawn here raise the permeability of endothelium almost as strongly as the cysteinyl leukotrienes, and human eosinophils and mast cells make them (Feltenmark 2008). Eoxin and 15-HETE levels are higher in asthmatic children than in healthy ones and follow bronchial hyperresponsiveness, which makes this branch a candidate marker in childhood asthma (Sachs-Olsen 2010).

In the lung vessels the same product acts on the muscle: 15-HETE made under low oxygen carries the constriction, the growth of smooth muscle and the remodelling of hypoxic pulmonary hypertension (Ma 2011).
