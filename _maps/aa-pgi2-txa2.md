Prostacyclin synthase (PTGIS) makes PGI2 from PGH2, mainly in the endothelium, and thromboxane synthase (TBXAS1) makes TXA2 and 12(S)-HHTrE, mainly in platelets. Both products are unstable in water. PGI2 hydrolyses to 6-keto-PGF1α, which gives 6-keto-PGE1 and the urinary 2,3-dinor-6-keto-PGF1α, and TXA2 hydrolyses to TXB2, whose 11-dehydro and 2,3-dinor forms are measured in urine. HPGD oxidises PGI2 to 15-oxo-PGI2. Acting on PGG2, the purified synthases give the 15-hydroperoxy forms of PGI2 and TXA2. In cells the cyclooxygenase reduces PGG2 to PGH2 first, so these forms are a minor side route. The cyclooxygenase step from PGG2 to PGH2, drawn in grey, belongs to the PGE2 map.
