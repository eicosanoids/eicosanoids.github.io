The cytochrome P450 product 20-HETE acts on GPR75, the first receptor found for an eicosanoid of this class (Garcia 2017). Binding of 20-HETE to the receptor releases the Gq alpha subunit and raises inositol phosphates, and from there the pathway runs through c-Src to the epidermal growth factor receptor and raises angiotensin-converting enzyme. In smooth muscle the same pairing works through protein kinase C on the MaxiK beta subunit, which links the receptor to the vasoconstriction 20-HETE causes. Mice without the receptor do not develop the blood pressure rise that 20-HETE drives.
