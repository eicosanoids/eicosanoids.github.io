15-lipoxygenase makes 13(S)-HOTrE from α-linolenic acid, and activated human T cells release 9- and 13-HOTrE as their main oxylipins (von Gerichten 2021). The 9 product is made by cytochrome P450, mostly CYP2B6, and not by a lipoxygenase (Evans 2024). 12-lipoxygenase makes 12-HpOTrE, and P450 epoxygenases make the EpODEs, for which no primary paper names the enzyme.
