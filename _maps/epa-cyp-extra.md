Cytochrome P450 enzymes hydroxylate eicosapentaenoic acid at its end and one carbon in, giving 20-HEPE and 19-HEPE, as they do with arachidonic acid.

Both steps are measured with recombinant human enzymes: CYP4F3 (both forms) and CYP4F2 are the main ω-hydroxylases of EPA, giving 20-HEPE, while CYP1A1, CYP2C19 and CYP2E1 hydroxylate mainly the carbon before it, giving 19-HEPE (Fer 2008).
