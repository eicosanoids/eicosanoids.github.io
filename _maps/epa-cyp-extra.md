Cytochrome P450 enzymes hydroxylate eicosapentaenoic acid at its end and one carbon in, giving 20-HEPE and 19-HEPE, as they do with arachidonic acid.

Both steps are predicted and drawn with dashed arrows. The enzymes that make 20-HETE from arachidonic acid carry these reactions in database entries and in a review, and no primary paper measures them on EPA.
