Two PGD synthases, lipocalin-type PTGDS and haematopoietic HPGDS, isomerise PGH2 to PGD2. Without enzymes PGD2 dehydrates to PGJ2, then Δ12-PGJ2 and 15-deoxy-Δ12,14-PGJ2. The microsomal glutathione transferase MGST3 conjugates 15-deoxy-Δ12,14-PGJ2 with glutathione in macrophages, and the conjugate is trimmed to a cysteine conjugate, which human mast cells release after IgE stimulation (Steinmetz-Späh 2022). AKR1C3 reduces PGD2 to 11β-PGF2α and back. HPGD inactivates PGD2 to 15-keto-PGD2, reduced further to its 13,14-dihydro form. Tetranor-PGDM and 2,3-dinor-11β-PGF2α are urinary metabolites. Purified rat PGD synthase acting on PGG2 gave a product presumed to be 15-hydroperoxy-PGD2. It was never identified, and the step is drawn dashed. The cyclooxygenase step from PGG2 to PGH2, drawn in grey, belongs to the PGE2 map.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. PGD2 is raised in the bald scalp of men with androgenetic alopecia and holds back hair follicle growth through the DP2 receptor (Garza 2012), but an oral DP2 antagonist gave no benefit over placebo in a trial, so the mechanism did not carry into treatment (DuBois 2021). In asthma, antagonism of the same receptor with fevipiprant gave small improvements, larger in patients with high eosinophil counts (Yang 2021).

The J series carries its own question. 15-Deoxy-PGJ2 is the ligand that gave this branch its link to PPARgamma, and it is also the step most often doubted, because it is a minor product that does not rise where that activation is claimed. The feedback map of this atlas draws that loop green for the same reason.
