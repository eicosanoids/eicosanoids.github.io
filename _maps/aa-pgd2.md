Two PGD synthases, lipocalin-type PTGDS and haematopoietic HPGDS, isomerise PGH2 to PGD2. Without enzymes PGD2 dehydrates to PGJ2, then Δ12-PGJ2 and 15-deoxy-Δ12,14-PGJ2. AKR1C3 reduces PGD2 to 11β-PGF2α and back. HPGD inactivates PGD2 to 15-keto-PGD2, reduced further to its 13,14-dihydro form. Tetranor-PGDM and 2,3-dinor-11β-PGF2α are urinary metabolites. PTGDS acting on PGG2 gives 15-hydroperoxy-PGD2. The cyclooxygenase step from PGG2 to PGH2, drawn in grey, belongs to the PGE2 map.
