LTC4 synthase (LTC4S), and microsomal glutathione transferase 2 (MGST2) in some cells, conjugate LTA4 with glutathione to make LTC4 inside the cell. The transporters ABCC1 (MRP1) and ABCC6 carry it out. Outside the cell, gamma-glutamyl transferases (GGT1, GGT5) remove glutamate to give LTD4, and the dipeptidases DPEP1 and DPEP2 remove glycine to give LTE4, the stable form measured in urine.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. This branch has both a drug and a marker in daily use. The CysLT1 antagonist montelukast improves lung function and symptoms in chronic asthma, independently of inhaled steroids (Reiss 1996). Leukotriene E4 in urine, the stable end of the chain, identifies aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease (Hagan 2017), which is why the inactivation map of this atlas keeps the urinary route beside the metabolic ones.
