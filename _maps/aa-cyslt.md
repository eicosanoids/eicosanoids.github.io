LTC4 synthase (LTC4S), and microsomal glutathione transferase 2 (MGST2) in some cells, conjugate LTA4 with glutathione to make LTC4 inside the cell. The transporters ABCC1 (MRP1) and ABCC6 carry it out. Outside the cell, gamma-glutamyl transferases (GGT1, GGT5) remove glutamate to give LTD4, and the dipeptidases DPEP1 and DPEP2 remove glycine to give LTE4, the stable form measured in urine.
