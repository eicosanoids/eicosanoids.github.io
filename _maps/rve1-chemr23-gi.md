Resolvin E1, made from eicosapentaenoic acid, binds ChemR23 (CMKLR1) and through it blocks nuclear factor kappaB and lowers interleukin 12 in dendritic cells (Arita 2005). The receptor is the one chemerin uses, and work with pertussis toxin and receptor-deficient cells places it on Gi (Hart 2010). Resolvin E1 also binds the leukotriene B4 receptor BLT1 as a partial agonist, so one mediator damps a leukotriene signal at one receptor and starts a resolution signal at another.
