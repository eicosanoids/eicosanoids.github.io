The cannabinoid receptor CB2 sits mainly on immune cells, first found on macrophages of the spleen (Munro 1993). 2-Arachidonoylglycerol binds it (Mechoulam 1995) and is its most potent natural agonist, while anandamide is only a weak partial agonist there (Sugiura 2000). In HL-60 cells the response is lost after pertussis toxin, which places CB2 on a Gi or Go protein. Through CB2, 2-AG reaches the cells that also make prostaglandins and leukotrienes in inflammation.
