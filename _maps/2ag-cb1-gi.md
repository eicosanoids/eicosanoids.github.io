2-Arachidonoylglycerol (2-AG) is the most abundant endocannabinoid and a full agonist of the cannabinoid receptor CB1, which is the receptor most common in the brain (Sugiura 1999). CB1 inhibits adenylyl cyclase through a pertussis toxin sensitive G protein of the Gi family (Matsuda 1990, Howlett 2002). 2-AG carries arachidonic acid. Monoacylglycerol lipase splits it into arachidonic acid and glycerol, and in brain this is the main supply of arachidonic acid for prostaglandins, so one molecule is both a CB1 agonist and a source of eicosanoids.
