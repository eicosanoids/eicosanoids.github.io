Aspirin-acetylated COX-2 turns EPA into 18R-HpEPE, reduced to 18-HEPE, the precursor of the E-series resolvins. In neutrophils 5-lipoxygenase adds a 5-hydroperoxy group and forms a 5,6-epoxide that LTA4 hydrolase opens to resolvin E1, or reduces it to resolvin E2 (Tjonahen 2006). Eosinophil 12/15-lipoxygenase makes resolvin E3 (Isobe 2012), and 15-lipoxygenases with 5-lipoxygenase make resolvin E4 in M2 macrophages. From 18-HEPE, 5-lipoxygenase also makes 8,18-diHEPE and ALOX12 makes 12,18-diHEPE, and 5-lipoxygenase oxygenates resolvin E3 further to 5,17,18-triHEPE (Kutzner 2020). HPGD inactivates resolvin E1 to 18-oxo-resolvin E1, and human neutrophils hydroxylate it at carbon 20 (Arita 2006).

No human lipoxygenase makes 18-HEPE in useful amounts: recombinant human enzymes give it at about 1% of their products, with little stereocontrol, so aspirin-acetylated COX-2 remains the drawn source and the endogenous one is open (Kutzner 2020, Kahnt 2023). 15-lipoxygenase-2 (ALOX15B) is drawn beside ALOX15 on resolvin E3, which it makes best among the human lipoxygenases, and on resolvin E4, the 5S,15S-dihydroxy product it forms with 5-lipoxygenase (Kutzner 2020, Kahnt 2023). Of the E-series, resolvin E4 is the one found by independent laboratories in human M2 macrophages (Hofstetter 2026).

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for recovery. Resolvin E1 is the one mediator of this map with receptor evidence from knockout animals: it acts as a partial agonist at the leukotriene B4 receptor BLT1, its low-dose action is lost in mice without that receptor, and it protects in experimental colitis and peritonitis (Arita 2007, Arita 2005). In localised aggressive periodontitis, patients make more leukotriene B4 and less lipoxin A4 and 14-HDHA and their macrophages take up poorly, a measured human failure of resolution that resolvin E1 reverses (Fredman 2011, Hasturk 2015).

The counterweight belongs on this page. Whether these mediators reach working concentrations in people is disputed: human leukocytes make the trihydroxylated ones at very low or undetectable levels because 5-lipoxygenase barely epoxidises the hydroxy precursors (Kahnt 2023, Mainka 2022), volunteers on fish oil showed no consistent signal in plasma or urine (Skarke 2015), and several groups hold that many published detections rest on methods below current standards or on autoxidation artefacts (Schebb 2022, Schebb 2026). In sepsis patients the measured mediators, resolvin E1 among them, were higher in those who died than in those who lived (Dalli 2017). The contested steps of the production maps are drawn green for the same reason.
