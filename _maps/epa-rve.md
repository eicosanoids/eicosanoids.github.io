Aspirin-acetylated COX-2 turns EPA into 18R-HpEPE, reduced to 18-HEPE, the precursor of the E-series resolvins. In neutrophils 5-lipoxygenase adds a 5-hydroperoxy group and forms a 5,6-epoxide that LTA4 hydrolase opens to resolvin E1, or reduces it to resolvin E2 (Tjonahen 2006). Eosinophil 12/15-lipoxygenase makes resolvin E3 (Isobe 2012), and 15-lipoxygenase makes resolvin E4 in M2 macrophages. HPGD inactivates resolvin E1 to 18-oxo-resolvin E1, and human neutrophils hydroxylate it at carbon 20 (Arita 2006).
