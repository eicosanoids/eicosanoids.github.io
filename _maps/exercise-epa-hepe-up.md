An exercise in turning a map: the same content as the map "12- and 15-lipoxygenase products of eicosapentaenoic acid" in the EPA network, its flow running bottom to top. The turned maps are made from the first one by the layout skill's orientation step, with no hand edits, so a piece can be joined to a large map in whichever direction the large map needs.

Reading order is kept: the first branch on the left of the downward map is the top branch of the maps running right or left. Steps along the flow are 200 px apart on a vertical flow and 300 px on a horizontal one, with room for long names, and branches 300 px apart on a vertical flow and 240 px on a horizontal one, with room for a catalyst on each side of a step. Catalysts sit beside their step on a vertical flow and above and below it on a horizontal one, their near edge 64 px from the step. Names stay horizontal.

The biology is described on the map in the EPA network.
