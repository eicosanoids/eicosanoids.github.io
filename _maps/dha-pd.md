15-lipoxygenase turns 17S-HpDHA into a 16S,17S-epoxide, which opens to protectin D1 (neuroprotectin D1), or oxygenates it a second time at carbon 10 to give protectin DX, a stereoisomer made without the epoxide (Serhan 2006, Tsai 2021). The epoxide is also conjugated with glutathione to PCTR1, which γ-glutamyl transferase and a dipeptidase trim to PCTR2 and PCTR3. From 17R-HpDHA, the aspirin-triggered route gives the 17R epimer of protectin D1.

One predicted step is drawn with a dashed arrow: the hydroxylation of protectin D1 at carbon 22. The product is measured and the enzyme is open, as mice lacking the Cyp1 enzymes do not make less of it.

Protectin D1 and what comes from its epoxide are drawn in green as contested: no enzyme has been shown to make protectin D1 independently of the original work, its structure was first assigned to a soybean lipoxygenase product that later studies read as a different isomer, and chiral analysis of human macrophages finds other 10,17-dihydroxy isomers instead (Schebb 2026, Hofstetter 2026). Protectin DX, made by two lipoxygenations, is found by several laboratories and stays solid. In M2 macrophages PCTRs form when LTC4 synthase is blocked, so a glutathione transferase other than LTC4 synthase makes them in cells (Liening 2019). The green steps are the epoxide, protectin D1, its aspirin-triggered form, its 22-hydroxy product and the PCTR conjugates.

## Relevance to disease

Where to look at this pathway from, for disease. Protectin D1 is measurable in human exhaled breath and falls during asthma exacerbations, and eosinophils from patients with severe asthma make much less of it even when they are given docosahexaenoic acid, which is the best human evidence of a deficit in this branch (Levy 2007, Miyata 2013). It also blocks influenza virus replication through the RNA export machinery, and its production is suppressed in severe infection (Morita 2013).

Whether these mediators are made at working concentrations in people is disputed, and the contested steps of this map are drawn green with their reasons. The dispute is summarised on the E-series resolvin page.
