LTB4 binds BLT1 (LTB4R), the high-affinity receptor of leukocytes (Yokomizo 1997). The receptor couples to Gi, whose α subunit lowers cyclic AMP, and to Gα16, and not to Gαq or Gα11 (Gaudreau 1998). Resolvin E1 binds the same receptor as a partial agonist and inhibits adenylyl cyclase through it, which is one way an ω-3 mediator damps LTB4 signalling (Arita 2007).

This map corrects the Starting map LTB4 receptors (BLT1 and BLT2), which drew BLT1 with Gq and phospholipase Cβ. The paper cited there rules out Gαq and Gα11.
